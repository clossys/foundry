SectionedView and LandingView now render `status-list` through catalog Items topics with localized status badges. Row labels become headings and details become descriptions. The original CopyRefs, provenance, and exactly-one state/disposition union remain; `not-offered` stays a separate neutral disposition. Status lookups require 3–8 flat entries or 2–4 groups of 2–4 entries, at most eight entries overall. This intentionally changes view markup and spacing: Items lists replace definition lists and the separate readiness legend. Standalone StatusList blocks remain available.
