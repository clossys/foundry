The `foundry` manifest block now declares `needs` and `solves` (package
framework, issue #1172). `needs` is empty, and that is a claim: no
capability takes an input from another role. `solves` claims the
`strategist-unclear-direction` problem, measured by the strategy
traceability rate, backed by `positioning` and shown by the
`handoff-positioning-unresolved` case, at `designed` evidence.

Minor, not patch: these are new declared manifest fields that discovery and
the Advisor catalogue read. Nothing that already existed changes shape.
