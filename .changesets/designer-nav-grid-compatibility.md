NavGrid href cards use title-only accessible names, with supporting copy outside the anchor; published 0.7.1 names also included supporting copy. Ordinary cards keep a full-tile hit area. When descriptions contain controls, the separate title link has a token-backed minimum tap target and focus outline, and description controls keep their own hit areas and keyboard stops. Linked titles and decorative icons require noninteractive content.
