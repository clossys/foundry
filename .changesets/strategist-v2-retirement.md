The legacy brand-coverage, direction, apply and brand-facts CLI modes, JSON facts-directory input and legacy strategy-root fallback are removed. The bare positional CLI form remains grounding over findings. Use checkGrounding, readFindings, openStrategy/getBrief and buildCitationIndex for the replacement flows. Site identity belongs to Publisher. Existing engagement-context, packed brief contracts, scan options and traceability-rate behavior are preserved.
