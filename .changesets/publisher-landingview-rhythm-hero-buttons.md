`LandingView` body bands are roomier: `py-4xl`, and `py-5xl` from `tablet-lg`. The closing call to action stands one step above them at every width: `py-5xl`, and `py-6xl` from `tablet-lg`. Both the hero and the call to action are Designer `SectionIntro`: the hero at the display emphasis, the call to action at the section emphasis. A hero's document actions render as Designer primary button links (`buttonLinkClassName("primary", "lg")`); to open with a message only, omit the actions. `SectionedViewContent` gains `heroActions` (`"link"` is the default, or `"button"`).
