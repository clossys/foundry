## Direction
- Metric: none; Foundry is not sold.
- Price: free, under the MIT licence.
- Anchor: the time to build and keep your own agent prompts, checks and records.
- Never a price, seat or usage fee on the packages.

## Because
- The repository and packages are MIT licensed (finding:foundry-public-repo-facts)
- The anchor follows the difference: the system is the work saved (advantage.defensibility.differentiator@4)
