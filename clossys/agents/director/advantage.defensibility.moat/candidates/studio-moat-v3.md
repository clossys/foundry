## Direction
- Moat: the pinned records, checks and history each business builds up on the system.
- Mechanism: every pin makes the next page cheaper to ship and every strict check catches drift, so the system keeps making the right pages.
- Milestone: one business run from idea to live on its own.
- Not the code: the engine is MIT and open, so anyone can copy it; the moat is the records a business builds, not the engine.

## Because
