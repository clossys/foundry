### studio.messaging.pitch-paragraph
<!-- {"id":"studio.messaging.pitch-paragraph","bindings":[]} -->
Coding agents can now build design systems, copy and pages, so the question shifts from making things to knowing where they came from. Clossys Foundry gives each agent one job and a contract. Director writes down the direction, Designer picks visuals from trusted references, Writer writes copy that cites the brief and Publisher puts pages together from a frame and sections. Every choice is a slot with candidates and no more than one owner pin, kept as files in your repository. Clossys Studio lets you look through it all without editing anything; when you want a change, the agent that owns it makes it.

### studio.messaging.boilerplate-long
<!-- {"id":"studio.messaging.boilerplate-long","bindings":[]} -->
Clossys Foundry is a free, open-source, agent-operated design and publishing engine for teams that keep their product in an npm workspace and work with a coding agent. Director writes down direction as slots. Designer picks visuals from shadcn and trusted registries and notes each adaptation. Writer writes copy that cites the Director brief. Publisher puts pages together from a frame, ordered sections and bindings, and never writes or designs. Every slot keeps its candidates as files, with no more than one owner pin, so each page can show where its direction, design and copy came from. Clossys Studio is where you look through that work, and it does not edit records; changes go through the owning agent. The packages are MIT licensed and public on npm.
