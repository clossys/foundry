### studio.messaging.pitch-paragraph
<!-- {"id":"studio.messaging.pitch-paragraph","bindings":[]} -->
Coding agents can now build design systems, copy and pages, so the hard part moves from making things to knowing where they came from. Clossys Foundry gives each agent one job and a contract. Director records direction, Designer picks visuals from trusted references, Writer writes copy that cites the brief and Publisher assembles pages from a frame and sections. Every choice is a slot with candidates and no more than one owner pin, kept as files in your repository. Clossys Studio lets you inspect it all without editing anything; a change goes through the agent that owns it.

### studio.messaging.boilerplate-long
<!-- {"id":"studio.messaging.boilerplate-long","bindings":[]} -->
Clossys Foundry is a free, open-source, agent-operated design and publishing engine for teams that keep their product in an npm workspace and work with a coding agent. Director records direction as slots. Designer picks visuals from shadcn and trusted registries and records each adaptation. Writer writes copy that cites the Director brief. Publisher assembles pages from a frame, ordered sections and bindings, and never writes or designs. Every slot keeps its candidates as files, with no more than one owner pin, so each page can show where its direction, design and copy came from. Clossys Studio is the workspace for inspecting that work, and it does not edit records; changes go through the owning agent. The packages are MIT licensed and public on npm.
