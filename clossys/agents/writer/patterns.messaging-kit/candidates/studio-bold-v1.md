### studio.messaging.boilerplate-long
<!-- {"id":"studio.messaging.boilerplate-long","bindings":[]} -->
Clossys Foundry is a free, open-source, agent-operated design and publishing engine for teams that keep their product in an npm workspace and work with a coding agent. Director records direction as slots. Designer picks visuals from shadcn and trusted registries and records each adaptation. Writer writes copy that cites the Director brief. Publisher assembles pages from a frame, ordered sections and bindings, and never writes or designs. Every slot keeps its candidates as files, with no more than one owner pin, so every page shows where its direction, design and copy came from. Clossys Studio is the read-only workspace for inspecting that work; changes go through the owning agent. The packages are MIT licensed and public on npm.
