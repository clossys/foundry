# publication (publisher)

Taste criteria for the critic (R15.4): scored 1 to 5, never pass or fail.

## findability

- 1: A reader cannot guess where a page lives.
- 2: Main pages are findable; the rest are buried.
- 3: Most pages are one guess away.
- 4: Every page is one guess away; one route name is internal.
- 5: Every page is where a newcomer would look first, under a name they would use.

## coherence

- 1: Routes mix several naming and nesting schemes.
- 2: One scheme with frequent exceptions.
- 3: One scheme with a few exceptions.
- 4: One scheme; one branch is deeper than it needs to be.
- 5: One naming and nesting scheme throughout, shallow where possible.

## route-naming

- 1: Route names are ids or internal jargon.
- 2: Readable names with inconsistent form.
- 3: Readable and consistent; one is ambiguous.
- 4: Readable, consistent and unambiguous; one is long.
- 5: Short, lowercase, plain-word routes a person could type from memory.
