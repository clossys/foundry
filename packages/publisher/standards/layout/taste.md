# layout (publisher)

Taste criteria for the critic (R15.4): scored 1 to 5, never pass or fail.

## region-order

- 1: Regions sit in no readable order; the main content is buried below secondary regions.
- 2: Header and footer are in place, but the main regions run in an arbitrary order.
- 3: A readable order with one region out of place.
- 4: Header, main and footer in order; one supporting region sits too early.
- 5: The frame's chrome, then the purpose, then each supporting region, then the close, in reading order.

## role-fit

- 1: Roles are filled by habit; most sections do not do their role's job.
- 2: Some roles fit, but several hold a section of the wrong kind.
- 3: Every role is filled by a fitting kind; one fits loosely.
- 4: Every role fits; one could use a closer section kind.
- 5: Each role is filled by the one section kind that does that job, and no role is padding.

## hierarchy

- 1: Every region carries equal weight; the reader cannot tell what the layout is for.
- 2: A lead exists but competes with another region.
- 3: A clear lead; the weight of the other regions is arbitrary.
- 4: A clear lead and order; one region is heavier than its job.
- 5: The layout leads with its purpose and every later region carries the weight its job needs.
