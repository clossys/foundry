# page (publisher)

Taste criteria for the critic (R15.4): scored 1 to 5, never pass or fail.

## hierarchy

- 1: Every section shouts; the reader cannot tell what matters.
- 2: A lead exists but competes with a second hero.
- 3: Clear lead; the supporting order is arbitrary.
- 4: Clear lead and order; one section sits too high.
- 5: The page leads with its purpose and every later section supports it in a sensible order.

## fit-to-purpose

- 1: Sections chosen by habit; half do not serve the page's job.
- 2: The job is served but with filler sections.
- 3: Every section serves the job; one is redundant.
- 4: Lean and on purpose; one needed section is missing.
- 5: Exactly the sections the page's job needs, nothing more.

## section-rhythm

- 1: Adjacent sections repeat the same layout and weight.
- 2: Some variety, but dense blocks cluster.
- 3: Readable rhythm with one heavy run.
- 4: Good rhythm; one transition is abrupt.
- 5: Dense and light sections alternate so the page reads at an even pace.
