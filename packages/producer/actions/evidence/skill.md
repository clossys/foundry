# evidence

Decide which owner answers and findings the Director may cite.

- Record what the owner said, dated, with its source; never a paraphrase that adds a claim.
- One answer per file, with frontmatter `id`, `source`, `observed` and `by`; the body is the answer.
- An answer that changes an earlier one supersedes it by a new file; the old file stays.
- The files are `clossys/evidence/director/*.md`; check:pipeline validates their frontmatter.
