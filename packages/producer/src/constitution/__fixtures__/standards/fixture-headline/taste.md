# fixture-headline taste

Test standard for the W2-I suite: a short product headline. The critic scores each criterion 1-5;
no score decides pass/fail (R15.4).

## clarity
- 1: The reader cannot say what the product does.
- 2: The claim is there but buried under qualifiers.
- 3: Clear on a second read.
- 4: Clear on first read, one idea.
- 5: Clear at a glance, and the idea is the reader's benefit.

## voice
- 1: Generic; could belong to any company.
- 2: Borrowed tone, mixed registers.
- 3: Consistent but flat.
- 4: Distinct and consistent with the brief.
- 5: Unmistakably the house voice, without strain.

## rhythm
- 1: Reads as a list of nouns.
- 2: Stumbles when read aloud.
- 3: Reads aloud without stumbling.
- 4: Has a natural stress pattern.
- 5: Memorable cadence; the last word lands.
