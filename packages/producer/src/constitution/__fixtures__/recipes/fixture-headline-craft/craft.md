# fixture-headline-craft

Test recipe for the W2-I suite. It makes headline candidates for the `fixture-headline` standard and
carries no rubric: the standard is the judge (R15.5, R16).

1. brief: read the slot contract, the brief and the taste log as preferences.
2. diverge: write 3-4 directions, each a distinct point on the axes tone, length and structure.
3. render: one candidate per direction.
4. critique: the critic scores the renders against taste.md and the exemplars, blind to this recipe.
5. refine: at most two rounds on the chosen direction.
