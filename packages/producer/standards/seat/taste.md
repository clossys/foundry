# seat (producer)

Taste criteria for the critic (R15.4): scored 1 to 5, never pass or fail.

## right-owner

- 1: The step is held by whoever was nearest, not by the agent or person it belongs to.
- 2: Plausible owner, but the step's output belongs to another agent.
- 3: Right owner; the human fallback is someone else without a reason.
- 4: Right owner and fallback; the boundary with the next step is implied.
- 5: The agent or person who owns the step's output holds it, and the boundary with neighbours is obvious.

## tier-fit

- 1: A judgment step on a low tier, or a mechanical step on high.
- 2: Tier is one level off for the step's risk.
- 3: Tier fits the step; the model choice is unexplained.
- 4: Tier and model fit; the cost of the step is not considered.
- 5: The lowest tier that is still sufficient for the step's risk, and nothing about it is surprising.

## procedure-clarity

- 1: The human procedure is a label, not a procedure.
- 2: A procedure a practitioner could follow with help.
- 3: A procedure a practitioner could follow; it misses the stop condition.
- 4: Complete; one step is ambiguous.
- 5: A newcomer could do the step by hand from the procedure and the named clossys command alone.
