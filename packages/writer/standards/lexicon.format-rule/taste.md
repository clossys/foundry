# lexicon.format-rule (writer)

Taste criteria for the critic (R15.4): scored 1 to 5, never pass or fail.

## voice-fit

- 1: Reads like generic marketing copy; nothing of the pinned voice.
- 2: Follows the voice's vocabulary but not its rhythm or stance.
- 3: Recognisably the pinned voice, with one off-tone line.
- 4: On voice throughout; one phrase is flatter than the voice allows.
- 5: Every line could only have come from the pinned voice, and the voice never gets in the way of the meaning.

## clarity

- 1: A reader must reread to learn what is meant or what to do.
- 2: Clear to an insider; jargon or internal names block a newcomer.
- 3: Clear on one reading; one phrase has two readings.
- 4: Clear on one reading; the order of ideas could be tighter.
- 5: One reading, plain words, the most important thing first.

## rule-usefulness

- 1: Rules restate taste (be consistent) with no instruction.
- 2: Instructions exist but no example shows them.
- 3: Instruction plus example; one rule conflicts with another.
- 4: Instruction plus example, no conflicts; one rule is rarely needed.
- 5: Each rule settles a real recurring choice and its example shows the exact form.
