# Review in Studio

This companion reserves the Studio review procedure. A complete Studio host
and adapter workflow is not established by this document. Ask for the
consumer's supported Studio host and verify its installed capabilities before
attempting a handoff; do not invent a route, port or save API.

Keep the chooser ChangeSet in a review basket. The owner reviews the proposed
changes, their rationale and overrides, signs promotions and decides whether
to save. Never auto-save. Keep the leaf identities stable while browsing a tree
view, and preserve rationale and overrides in the review record. Machine checks
do not replace the owner's visual gate, including light and dark review.
