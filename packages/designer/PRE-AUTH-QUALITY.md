# Pre-auth quality

A pre-auth page is not kept because Designer passed tokens or Writer passed
copy. Those prove the candidate was **made**. Keep is a different question:
if I am the person this was made for, do I keep this?

That keep is an independent first-person inhabit. Invoke `@clossys-customer`.
Do not fold it into Designer, Writer, Publisher, or Inspector. Do not hire a
QA contractor to tick fold, type, or voice boxes — those already proved 3.

Customer is not a checklist and not a five-star certifier. Dishonest
first-person still passes the inhabit shape. The person may also be asked,
in the same inhabit, for lived functional and experiential feedback, a
comparison from alternatives they actually know, what it would take to
refer, and what would make them churn. Those sessions are the same person
on speed dial. They are not a second charter metric and they are not a
reviewer.

## Sequence

1. Strategist — citable Audience and direction.
2. Designer and Writer — the candidate, in parallel.
3. `@clossys-customer` — independent session. Speak as the named person.
   Keep, or fail. Do not negotiate the keep with the doers.
4. Publisher — seal only after keep.

Inspector stays on PR rules in parallel. Observer measures after land.
Influencer measures real qualified responses, not this synthetic inhabit.
