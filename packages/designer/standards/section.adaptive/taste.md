# section.adaptive taste

A section that serves both surfaces (surface both): headers, bands, documentation and content blocks that appear on the site and in the app. It composes pinned components and parts by slot id and reads only pinned theme tokens.

The critic scores each criterion 1-5 against the exemplars; no score decides pass or fail (R15.4).

## hierarchy
- 1: No clear first read.
- 2: Competing elements.
- 3: Clear first read only.
- 4: Clear reading order.
- 5: The order matches the content's importance exactly.

## fit-both
- 1: Belongs to one surface only.
- 2: Works on both with visible compromise.
- 3: Works on both, at home on one.
- 4: At home on site and in app.
- 5: Indistinguishable from a section drawn for each surface.

## polish
- 1: Visible flaws.
- 2: Rough at breakpoints.
- 3: Clean at one width.
- 4: Clean at every width and mode.
- 5: Holds up beside the exemplars.
