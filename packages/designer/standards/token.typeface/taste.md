# token.typeface taste

A font family token (sans, heading, mono, serif) with the type scale it is read at. The shadcn font items and the Tailwind stacks are references; the choice realises the pinned Director style, personality and voice, and says which.

The critic scores each criterion 1-5 against the exemplars; no score decides pass or fail (R15.4).

## voice-fit
- 1: The face argues with the brand voice.
- 2: A neutral default chosen by habit.
- 3: Fits the voice but anonymous.
- 4: Its tone matches the pinned personality.
- 5: Reads as the brand speaking, even without the logo.

## legibility
- 1: Hard to read at body sizes.
- 2: Readable but tiring in long passages or dense UI.
- 3: Comfortable at body size only.
- 4: Clear from captions to display.
- 5: Effortless at every size the scale uses, in both modes.

## scale-rhythm
- 1: Sizes look arbitrary.
- 2: Steps too close to tell apart.
- 3: A regular scale with weak contrast at the top.
- 4: Clear steps with intentional tracking and leading.
- 5: The scale gives hierarchy with few sizes and the tracking tightens as size grows.

## pairing
- 1: Families clash.
- 2: Two families too similar to justify both.
- 3: Acceptable pair, no clear roles.
- 4: Each family has a role (UI, display, code).
- 5: The pair reads as one system with distinct jobs.
