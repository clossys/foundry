# asset.logo-mark taste

The brand mark, lockup, favicon and app icon. Drawn, never taken from a registry: each candidate derives from the pinned imagery direction and the brand essence or name it expresses, cites those Director slots, works in light and dark and survives 16px.

The critic scores each criterion 1-5 against the exemplars; no score decides pass or fail (R15.4).

## distinctiveness
- 1: Generic; could be any company.
- 2: Echoes a well-known mark.
- 3: Distinct but forgettable.
- 4: Memorable after one look.
- 5: Ownable: recognisable from its silhouette alone.

## reduction
- 1: Falls apart at favicon size.
- 2: Needs a separate small version and has none.
- 3: Survives small sizes with loss.
- 4: Holds from 16px to billboard.
- 5: Reduced to the fewest shapes that still carry the idea.

## brand-fit
- 1: Contradicts the brand essence.
- 2: Neutral to the brand.
- 3: Fits the style but not the idea.
- 4: Expresses the essence the reason cites.
- 5: The mark and the brand idea are the same thing.

## construction
- 1: Uneven curves and spacing.
- 2: Mostly clean, visible flaws up close.
- 3: Clean but unbalanced.
- 4: Built on a clear grid with optical corrections.
- 5: Every curve and gap is deliberate and the lockup spacing is a rule.
