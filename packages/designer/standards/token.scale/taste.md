# token.scale taste

A non-colour token on a scale: radius, spacing, shadow, tracking and motion. The Tailwind v4 theme values are the base; the candidate states the Director trait its value realises (for example a quiet and exact style asks for small radii, soft shadows and short motion).

The critic scores each criterion 1-5 against the exemplars; no score decides pass or fail (R15.4).

## rhythm
- 1: Steps look arbitrary.
- 2: Steps too close or too far apart.
- 3: Regular but mechanical.
- 4: Steps feel even in use.
- 5: The ramp gives every layout a steady beat.

## density
- 1: The value fights the product's density.
- 2: Too loose for an app or too tight for a page.
- 3: Works in one surface only.
- 4: Suits both app and site at their sizes.
- 5: Density matches the brand: calm, never cramped.

## coherence
- 1: Contradicts the other scale tokens.
- 2: Matches some tokens, not others.
- 3: Consistent but generic.
- 4: Radius, shadow and motion agree on one temperament.
- 5: Every scale token reads as one decision.
