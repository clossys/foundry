# asset.media-rule taste

Rules for imagery, video, aspect ratios and patterns: what a page may show and how. Each candidate derives from the pinned imagery direction (director:identity.visual.imagery) and states the rule, never a stock picture.

The critic scores each criterion 1-5 against the exemplars; no score decides pass or fail (R15.4).

## imagery-fit
- 1: Rules allow imagery that contradicts the direction.
- 2: Rules are silent on subject and tone.
- 3: Fits the direction loosely.
- 4: Subject, tone and treatment follow the direction.
- 5: Any image the rule admits would look like this brand.

## restraint
- 1: Rules invite decoration.
- 2: Too many patterns or ratios.
- 3: A reasonable set, one or two too many.
- 4: Few ratios and patterns, each with a use.
- 5: The smallest set that covers every page.

## consistency
- 1: Rules conflict with each other.
- 2: Gaps leave choices to chance.
- 3: Consistent within one rule.
- 4: All media rules agree.
- 5: Media, ratios and patterns read as one system with the tokens.
