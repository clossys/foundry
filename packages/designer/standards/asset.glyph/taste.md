# asset.glyph taste

An interface glyph on the 24px grid. The icon library is a source; the candidate derives from the pinned imagery direction (director:identity.visual.imagery) and stays legible at 16px in both modes.

The critic scores each criterion 1-5 against the exemplars; no score decides pass or fail (R15.4).

## stroke-harmony
- 1: Stroke weights and caps differ from the set.
- 2: Mostly consistent with visible outliers.
- 3: Consistent but heavy or thin for the UI type.
- 4: Matches the set and the UI type weight.
- 5: Indistinguishable in weight and finish from the rest of the set.

## optical-balance
- 1: Visibly off-centre or oversized.
- 2: Fills the grid unevenly.
- 3: Centred but optically heavy.
- 4: Optically centred and sized to its neighbours.
- 5: Sits on the baseline beside text as if drawn for it.

## small-legibility
- 1: Unreadable at 16px.
- 2: Recognisable only in context.
- 3: Readable at 16px with effort.
- 4: Clear at 16px.
- 5: Clear at 16px and its meaning is unmistakable.
