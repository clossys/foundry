# section.site taste

A marketing or brand section (surface site): hero, features, proof, pricing, footer bands. It composes pinned components and section parts by slot id, reads only pinned theme tokens and takes its copy from Writer. The bar is Linear, Vercel, OpenAI and Stripe.

The critic scores each criterion 1-5 against the exemplars; no score decides pass or fail (R15.4).

## hierarchy
- 1: No clear first read.
- 2: Several elements compete for attention.
- 3: A first read, but the second is unclear.
- 4: First, second and third reads are clear.
- 5: The eye moves exactly as the message needs.

## rhythm
- 1: Spacing is uneven.
- 2: Bands feel stacked, not paced.
- 3: Regular but monotonous.
- 4: Space varies with purpose.
- 5: The page breathes; every gap is on the scale and meant.

## restraint
- 1: Decoration everywhere.
- 2: Effects compete with the message.
- 3: Mostly restrained with one excess.
- 4: Only what the message needs.
- 5: Nothing to remove; quiet and exact.

## polish
- 1: Visible misalignments and states missing.
- 2: Aligned but rough at breakpoints.
- 3: Clean at desktop only.
- 4: Clean at every breakpoint and in both modes.
- 5: Holds up beside the exemplars at every width.
